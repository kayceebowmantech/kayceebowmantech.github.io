ABOUT ME

I like figuring out how things work.

I've been interested in programming since taking an online JavaScript class in high school and watching people build and program robots. Growing up as an avid gamer also made me curious about how the things I enjoyed were actually made.

What I like most about programming is being able to take complicated ideas in my head and turn them into something real and interactive. There's something satisfying about starting with an idea and eventually seeing it actually work.

When I get stuck, I usually retrace my steps to figure out where things stopped working, research the problem, and keep testing different solutions until I understand it.

I also enjoy the visual side of creating. I like working with scenes, characters, assets, and overall presentation, and I'm interested in combining those skills with programming and game development.
