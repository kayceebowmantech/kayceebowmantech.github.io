ABOUT ME

I like figuring out how things work.

I've been interested in programming since I took a JavaScript class online in high school and started watching people build and program robots. Growing up as an avid gamer also made me curious about how the things I enjoyed were actually made.

What I like most about programming is being able to take complicated ideas in my head and turn them into something real and interactive. There's something satisfying about starting with an idea and eventually seeing it actually work.

I'm naturally curious and analytical, so when something doesn't work, I usually retrace my steps to figure out where I got stuck, research the problem, and keep testing different solutions until I understand it.
